You can play volleyball in Bangkok in four ways. Book a single spot in a pay-per-game session, join a community group that splits the court fee, rent a court for your own group, or use the free public courts in Benjakitti Park. The right one depends on your level, whether you already have people to play with, and how much organising you want to do.

*We run Playco, one of the options below, so we are not neutral. We have described the others the way their own pages do, and linked each one so you can check. Last checked: 24 November 2026.*

## Which one should you pick?

- **New in town, no team, want a game this week:** pay per game. You book one spot and the organiser sorts the teams.
- **Experienced indoor player who wants a hard game:** the competitive men's indoor session run by BKK Volleyball.
- **Comfortable at intermediate level and free on Tuesdays:** Bangkok Volley.
- **Already have a dozen people:** rent a court and split it.
- **Just want to touch a ball:** the free public courts.

## How does pay-per-game pickup work?

You pick a game, pay for one spot and show up. At Playco a spot costs 300 THB for a two-hour game, paid by card or PromptPay when you book. We rent the court, bring the balls and send an organiser who welcomes people, splits the teams and keeps time.

We play in two formats. The beach game is 5v5 on an indoor sand court at Asoke Sports Club, on the eleventh floor of the Foodland building at 48 Soi Sukhumvit 16, about five minutes from Asok BTS and Sukhumvit MRT. The indoor game is 6v6 at Swish Elite 50, where three teams of six rotate. It is at 605 Sukhumvit Soi 50 in Phra Khanong, and On Nut is the nearest BTS station.

Games are open to all levels unless the listing shows a level badge. If a game is full you can join the waitlist. If you cancel at least 12 hours before the start, the money comes back as credit on your account. [This week's games are here](/games), and [what to expect at your first game](/blog/first-pickup-volleyball-game-bangkok) covers the rest.

## What are the community groups like?

**BKK Volleyball Social Group** is the big one: about 2,700 members on Meetup and more than a hundred sessions scheduled ahead. When we checked, it ran three kinds of session at weekends. Saturday afternoon is sand volleyball for all levels. Sunday has a beach 4x4 for intermediate and advanced players, and a competitive men's indoor session at Virgin Active 101 on Sukhumvit Road. The sand sessions are at Arsenal Soccer School on Soi Pridi Banomyong 21 in Phra Khanong.

There is no fixed price. The group splits the court fee between whoever plays. Its page gives the sand court at 800 THB an hour, or 1,000 THB with lights, and adds 5 to 20 THB a head for balls at the indoor session. The indoor session expects you to know the standard rules and rotation, and indoor shoes are required.

**Bangkok Volley** is a smaller club on Reclub, an app for organising sports sessions, with 188 members. It describes itself as a club for players at least at intermediate level and plays on Tuesdays, with extra days announced in the app. Its public page showed no upcoming sessions when we looked, so check the app.

## Can you rent a volleyball court in Bangkok?

Yes, and it works if you can reliably bring ten or more people. BKK Volleyball's listing is a useful price marker: 800 to 1,000 THB an hour for a sand court. Asoke Sports Club has sand courts as well as pickleball; ask the venue about hiring one.

The catch is the organising. Someone has to book the court, collect the money and find a twelfth player when two drop out on the day.

## Where are the free volleyball courts in Bangkok?

Benchakitti Sports Center, the public sports complex in Benjakitti Park beside the Queen Sirikit National Convention Centre, is free to use. It is known for its ten free pickleball courts. Visitors also report volleyball there, alongside basketball, badminton and table tennis. There are no organised games, so bring a ball and enough people. Go and look before you plan a session around it.

## Beach or indoor?

Sand is softer to land on and the game is slower. With five a side you also touch the ball more often. Indoor is faster, with set positions and harder hitting. If you have not played since school, the sand game is the gentler way back in.

## Questions people ask

**Can beginners play volleyball in Bangkok?**
Yes. Playco games are open to all levels unless marked, and BKK Volleyball lists its Saturday sand session for all levels. The competitive indoor sessions and Bangkok Volley expect experience, so start with a casual game and move up when it feels easy.

**Do I need a team or a partner?**
No. Pay-per-game sessions and the Meetup group both take individuals. You only need your own group if you rent a court or use the free public courts.

**How much does it cost to play?**
From nothing at the public courts to 300 THB for a two-hour organised game. Splitting a court is cheaper per head when enough people come: 800 THB an hour between 14 players is under 60 THB each. Playco passes bring a game down to between 220 and 280 THB.

**Is there volleyball near Asok or Sukhumvit?**
Yes. Asoke Sports Club is on Sukhumvit Soi 16, a short walk from Asok BTS and Sukhumvit MRT, and Benjakitti Park is close by. Virgin Active 101 and the Phra Khanong sand courts are further east along the Sukhumvit line.

**What should I bring?**
Indoor court shoes for indoor games, water and a towel. Balls are provided at organised sessions, so you only need your own for the public courts. Take off watches and jewellery before you play.
