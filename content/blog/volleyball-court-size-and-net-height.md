An indoor volleyball court is 18 metres long and 9 metres wide. A beach volleyball court is 16 by 8 metres. In both, the top of the net is 2.43 metres for men and 2.24 metres for women. These are the FIVB's official measurements; the tables below give them in metres and feet.

## How big is a volleyball court?

| | Indoor | Beach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Court | 18 m by 9 m (59 ft by 29 ft 6 in) | 16 m by 8 m (52 ft 6 in by 26 ft 3 in) |
| Each team's half | 9 m by 9 m | 8 m by 8 m |
| Attack line | 3 m from the centre line | None |
| Clear space around the court | At least 3 m on every side | At least 3 m on every side |
| Players a side | 6 | 2 |

The lines are part of the court. A ball that touches a line is in.

## How high is a volleyball net?

| | Height at the centre | In feet and inches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Men | 2.43 m | 7 ft 11⅝ in |
| Women | 2.24 m | 7 ft 4⅛ in |

The height is measured at the centre of the court. Beach volleyball uses the same two heights. Recreational and mixed games are not bound by them, so ask the organiser what the net is set to.

## What is the attack line for?

The attack line runs across each half of an indoor court, three metres from the centre line. It separates the front zone from the back. A back-row player may not attack the ball from above net height while in front of it. [How rotation works](/blog/volleyball-rotation-explained) explains who counts as back row.

Beach courts have no attack line, because with two players a side there is no back row.

## How big is the ball?

| | Indoor | Beach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Circumference | 65 to 67 cm | 66 to 68 cm |
| Weight | 260 to 280 g | 260 to 280 g |
| Feel | Firmer | Softer, at lower pressure |

The two weigh the same. The beach ball is a little larger and softer, which slows it down in the air.

## How much space do you need to set up a court?

For a full indoor court with its clear space, plan for about 24 by 15 metres. For a beach court, about 22 by 14 metres. A casual game fits in less, as long as there is room behind the back line to serve and nothing hard close to the sidelines.

## Questions people ask

**Is a beach volleyball court smaller than an indoor court?**
Yes. It is two metres shorter and one metre narrower: 16 by 8 metres against 18 by 9. Two players cover it instead of six, and sand is slower to move on.

**How high is the net for mixed volleyball?**
The official rules give heights for men and women only. Mixed leagues set their own. For a casual game, ask the organiser.

**What are the lines on a volleyball court?**
Two sidelines, two end lines, the centre line under the net, and on an indoor court an attack line on each side. The server stands behind the end line.

**How many square metres is a volleyball court?**
An indoor court is 162 square metres. A beach court is 128 square metres. Add the clear space around it and an indoor court needs about 360 square metres.
