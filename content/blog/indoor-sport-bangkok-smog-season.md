When PM2.5 climbs, the simplest fix is to move your exercise indoors. Bangkok has more air-conditioned places to play than most people expect, including a sand court eleven floors above Sukhumvit. This is a short list of the ones we can point to a source for, with what they cost and how to join.

## When is Bangkok's smog season?

Fine-dust pollution builds up in the cool, dry months, when still air holds it close to the ground. It usually sets in around December and eases in March. In 2026 the Pollution Control Department said on 11 March that the capital was past the worst.

Readings can climb before December. On 15 November 2026 The Nation reported that the department expected levels around Bangkok to rise on 19 and 20 November as colder air settled over the city.

Check the day's reading before you exercise outside. The Pollution Control Department and the Bangkok Air Quality Information Centre both publish forecasts.

## What is indoor beach volleyball?

It is beach volleyball on real sand, under a roof. Asoke Sports Club has its sand courts inside, on the eleventh floor of the Foodland building on Sukhumvit Soi 16, about five minutes from Asok BTS and Sukhumvit MRT. THE STANDARD described it as beach volleyball in cool air-conditioning in the middle of the city.

We run a 5v5 game there. It is the one to pick on a bad-air day if you have never played on sand: the landing is soft and the pace is slower than the indoor game.

## Does the same list work in the heat and the rain?

Yes. The hot months, roughly March to May, and the rains, roughly May to October, cancel outdoor games for different reasons. Indoor courts do not care which. All our current games are under a roof.

## Is it ever worth playing outside?

On a clear day, yes. Benchakitti Sports Center in Benjakitti Park is free and has public courts. Look at the reading first, and keep an indoor option for the days it is high.

## Questions people ask

**Do I need to book indoor games in advance?**
For organised games, yes. Playco spots are booked and paid online, and the Meetup groups work by RSVP. Book ahead rather than on the day, so the organiser knows the numbers and you are sure of a place.

**Do I need experience?**
Not for the casual sessions. Playco games are open to all levels unless marked, and Bangkok Meetups Pickleball describes its events as suitable from beginner to intermediate. The competitive indoor volleyball session expects you to know the rules.

**What should I wear indoors?**
Clean indoor court shoes with non-marking soles for hard courts, and light sports kit. Bring water and a towel. On the sand court, check with the organiser before your first game.
