You book one spot online, arrive ten minutes early with court shoes and water, get put in a team by the organiser, and play for two hours. You do not need a team, a partner or much experience. The rest of this page is the detail, including what happens if you have to cancel.

## Do I need to be good?

No. All levels are welcome, and games are casual unless the listing shows a level badge. The level on your profile is your own estimate. It is shown to other players but nobody checks it.

If you are new or rusty, say so when you arrive. The organiser splits the teams, and an even game is better for everyone.

## How does booking work?

1. Pick a game on the [games page](/games).
2. Pay for your spot by card or PromptPay. A spot only exists once it is paid.
3. The site shows the booking as confirmed and you get an email.

There is no paying in cash at the court. You need to be 18 or over to have an account. Where the listing allows it, you can book extra spots for friends.

## What happens at the court?

Arrive ten minutes before the start. An organiser is there to welcome people, split the teams and keep time. The organiser is not a referee or a coach, so players call their own points and settle disagreements calmly between themselves.

A game lasts 120 minutes. Indoors we play 6v6 with three teams of six rotating, so everyone gets a short rest between games. On sand we play 5v5. The organiser explains on the day how the teams swap on and off.

Games are run in English and Thai.

## What should I bring?

- Indoor court shoes for the indoor game. For the sand court, ask the organiser before your first game.
- Water and a towel.
- Nothing else. We bring the volleyballs.

Take off watches and jewellery before you play. Both venues have showers and drinks available, and Swish Elite 50 has parking.

## What if I can't make it?

Cancel on the site at any time before the start. If you cancel at least 12 hours ahead, you get a full refund to your account: card and PromptPay payments come back as credit you can spend on any game, and pass credits go back to your pass. Cancel later than that, or do not show up, and there is no refund.

If you would rather have the money than the credit, email support@playllu.com. The organiser or the city manager reviews the request, and an approved refund goes back the way you paid.

We sometimes have to cancel a game ourselves, for example when too few people have booked or the venue cannot give us the court. You get the same full refund to your account, and we send the money back instead if you ask.

## What if the game is full?

Join the waitlist. When a spot opens, everyone on the list is told by email and the first person to book gets it. This is also why cancelling early matters: it gives your spot to someone who is waiting.

## Is it strange to come alone?

No. You book a spot, not a team, so arriving on your own is the normal case.

## Questions people ask

**Can I bring a friend who has not signed up?**
Where the listing allows guests, you can book extra spots on your own account. You are responsible for the booking, so make sure your friend knows the time, the place and the basic rules.

**How early should I arrive?**
Ten minutes before the start. The court is booked for a fixed two hours, so a late start means less play for everyone, and arriving late does not earn a refund.

**What if I get hurt?**
Stop the game and get help. The organiser is not a medical professional, and Playco does not provide first aid. In an emergency in Thailand, call 1669. Do not play if your health does not allow it.

**Do I have to know all the rules?**
You need the basics: three touches a side, no catching the ball, rotate when your team wins the serve back. People explain the rest as you go, and nobody expects a newcomer to be perfect.
